Two risks you accepted

Both were reviewed, both are recorded in docs/ACCEPTED-RISKS.md, and both have a known fix. They are listed here so accepting them stays a decision rather than an oversight.

The shared-ride matching window

One database rule exists so the matching step can mark the other passenger's booking. It keys only on the booking being in its matching window, so a modified app could take over such a booking, and a driver whose approval is not yet granted could claim one.

Fix: move matching to a server-side function, or narrow the rule to the two fields it needs.

Six database nodes open to any signed-in user

Trusted contacts, pair requests, emergency alerts, chats, trip groups and match locks stay open because those flows cross users. The worst case is injecting yourself into another user's trusted contacts and then receiving their emergency alerts, including live location.

Fix: scope each node to the parties involved, then retest pairing and alerts.

What has not been proven

  • The app has never been run on a device or emulator. An attempt was made and is impossible on this machine: the only emulator profile points at an empty system image, and no hypervisor driver is installed.
  • The database rules have never been executed. They are written and staged, not deployed and not tested.
  • The minified release build has never been run, because it is unsigned and cannot be installed as produced.
