The three partial classes

Each is partial for a stated reason, not an unknown one.

  • Database rulesAuthored and indexed, but never deployed and never executed. Until the rules run, they close nothing. Partial
  • Full-node readsListeners are detached and the indexes cover the queries, but whole-node reads remain on the screens that compute counts over the full set. Partial
  • Image blobsProfile pictures moved to Storage, but receipts, discount ID images and driver documents are still stored inline as base64 because Storage is not enabled. Partial

What has not been proven

  • Nothing has been run on a device or emulator. An attempt is impossible on this machine: the only emulator profile points at an empty system image, and no hypervisor driver is installed.
  • The database rules have never been executed. They are authored and indexed, not deployed and not tested.
  • The minified release build has never been run, because it is unsigned and cannot be installed as produced.
  • The wallet commission path is the one pure-logic unit without a test; coverage stands at 6 of 7 units, which is 85.71 percent.
